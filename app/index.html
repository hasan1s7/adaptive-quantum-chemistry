      How to read this explorer
      The matched simple policy is the exact simple configuration selected on the training families for each fold. Fixed top-2 is a separate comparator. Comparing with one does not establish a result against the other.
      Success means reference regret at most 1 kcal/mol, with the benchmark’s numerical tolerance. “More calls, same regret” means a call increase with regret equal within 10⁻⁸ kcal/mol. “Early stop loses success” means fewer calls and a failed 1 kcal/mol decision where the comparator succeeded. These descriptive filters may overlap.
      Family-level uncertainty and the registered operating-point decision remain in Measured results. The exploratory subset summaries here are counts, not new confidence intervals, independent experiments, or policy validation.
