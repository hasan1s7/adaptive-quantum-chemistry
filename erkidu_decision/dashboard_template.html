What this comparison does—and does not—establish

A success selects a structure within 20 meV/atom of the lowest recorded reference energy in its composition pool. A “reference label” is a replayed DFT relaxation outcome, not a new single-point job. Inference and DFT costs were not measured; zero reference labels does not mean zero computation.

The bootstrap resamples chemical systems while retaining paired pools. It is conditional on this fit and roster; shared training data and structural lineage limit independence. These results do not establish prospective reliability or customer savings.

What the cost comparison means

All model predictions were imported. A future workflow would need one model for fixed MACE/M3GNet and three for the combined EI signal. Real relaxation, inference, queue and integration costs remain unmeasured.

The selection loss does not separately reward reference confirmation. If a buyer requires it, that becomes a different endpoint to freeze before a new evaluation.

Source convergence remains a limitation: the published prediction tables do not include per-job convergence flags, final forces or ionic-step counts. A finite energy does not establish a converged relaxation.
