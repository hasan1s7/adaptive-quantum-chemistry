05 / AUDIT & METHODS

Evidence before claims.

Definitions and protocol

Rescue = regret(top1) > 1 and regret(top2) ≤ 1 kcal/mol relative to the best comparison label among ten candidates. Train-only smoothed histogram: seven cheap-gap bins, 20 pseudo-observations at the training rescue rate. Shape methods use train-only medians by amplitude and path. Spectral proxy: degree-16 Chebyshev fit, power in degrees 3–16 divided by nonconstant power. MAC proxy: mean absolute second derivative with respect to configuration-space arc length; kcal/mol/Å². These definitions are not a replication of the original MLIP thesis.

Training classes: alkanes, alcohols, ethers, ketones, esters, amines, cycloalkanes, aromatics. Held-out: amides, nitriles, thioethers, haloalkanes. Class blocks are design categories, not certified disjoint scaffold families. Generated geometry has no third-party experimental labels. The holdout is new within this pilot only; four classes cannot establish population performance.

Measured train UFF 7.880 μs and MMFF94 9.001 μs per point; repeat cost ratio 0.879936. Timing excludes setup and feature extraction, depends on machine, and is not monetary cost or an xTB/DFT cost ratio. All 1,080 non-timing trajectory records reproduced exactly; six local component tests passed.

Original v0.2 sealed result unchanged: sequential 455/478, 973 calls; matched simple 457/478, 964 calls. No 30% matched-risk savings demonstrated. No MLIP training, quantum labels, degradation rates, MD validation or industrial claims.
