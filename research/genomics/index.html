When does interaction information pay?

19,200 simulated landscapes, 120 abstract pairs each. No biological sequences, real assay labels or validated genomic predictions. Four fixed-budget rankings: random, additive, uncertainty-only and optimistic score. The optimistic rule is a proxy, not expected value of information.

All policies pay 16 initial observations plus the pair budget. Success means regret at most 0.25 arbitrary units. Each condition uses 300 paired landscapes. Shift removes the relationship between predicted interaction scale and true variability. Pair measurements are exact; only initial observations are noisy.
